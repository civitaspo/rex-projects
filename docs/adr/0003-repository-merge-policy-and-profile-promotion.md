Each repository can describe its merge mechanism in merge instructions and instruction files. Merge Decision translates them into an ordered typed procedure for PR-body preparation, direct merge or automation request, and required merged-commit verification. The controller validates and executes each step with a stable effect receipt. A merged commit that fails post-merge verification blocks operations and ticket completion without another merge or automatic revert. Bind assessments and approvals to exact change revisions, the prepared body and expected trailers, instruction digests, and resolved policy. Instructions cannot waive required checks or execution-time head and base guards. GitHub's expected-head SHA alone does not guard the base; unattended execution also requires verified server-side base freshness or a registered executor with an equivalent guarantee.
